## 2. Synthesis

**Walk weights (access/egress/transfer walk, × IVT), calibrated regional models only (excluding template copies A9 and routing/MATSim defaults):**
- Tour-level values: MTC/TM1 2.0 (A1); SEMCOG 2.0 (B1); ARC 2.0 short / 2.5 long (work; 1.85–2.74 by purpose) (C1); MWCOG 1.83 access / 2.0 aux (work; 1.74–2.0) (D1–D2); SANDAG ABM3 2.0 access / 2.5 transfer walk (E1, E4); SimOR 2.0 / 2.5 (F1); PSRC ActivitySim 1.97 (work; 1.5–2.5 across adult purposes) (G1); SoundCast/SeaCast/PierceCast/BKRCast DaySim 2.0 (H1, H6–H8); Chattanooga 1.0 (H9). Assignment-level walk perception: SoundCast Emme 2.0 (1.5 CR), TM2 2.0, CMAP 2 access / 3 transfer-walk.
- **Count 10 independent model families; median 2.0; range 1.0–2.5 (long/transfer walk up to 2.5–3.0).** Only MTC (trip model) and SEMCOG escalate long walks for the walk *mode* (5–10×), not for transit access.

**Initial wait (× IVT):** MTC 2.0 (≤10 min) / 1.0 beyond (A2, I2); SEMCOG 2/1 (B2); ARC 2.0 (≤7.5 min and beyond; 1.33 nonmandatory) (C2); MWCOG 1.49–1.50 (D3); SANDAG 1.5 (E2); SimOR 1.5 (F1); PSRC ASim 1.5 with nonlinear decay (G2); DaySim Seattle family 2.0 (H2, H6–H8); Chattanooga 1.25; TM2 assignment 1.5 (J1–J2); CMAP 2 (skims) / 2.75 (assignment). **Count 10; median 1.5–2.0 (bimodal: 1.5 for the newer estimated models SANDAG/SimOR/PSRC/MWCOG/TM2; 2.0 for MTC/SEMCOG/ARC/DaySim); range 1.25–2.75.** Half-headway initial wait is explicit wherever an assignment config was readable (SoundCast `@hdwfr 0.5`, TM2 `headway_fraction 0.5` (0.1 for ferry/commuter rail), CMAP 0.5); caps: MTC/SEMCOG/MWCOG-trip weight drop to 1.0 beyond 10 min; SANDAG `max_waitTime 50` applies only to ridehail.

**Transfer wait (× IVT):** MTC 2.0; SEMCOG 2.0; ARC 1.5 (on XWAIT+XPEN); MWCOG 1.53–2.0; SANDAG 2.0; SimOR 2.0; PSRC ASim 2.0; DaySim 2.0–2.5; Chattanooga 1.25; TM2 assignment 3.0–3.5; CMAP assignment 3 (xfer_wait). **Median 2.0; range 1.25–3.5 (assignment-level values above 2 are higher than mode-choice values).**

**Transfer / boarding penalties (min IVT per transfer):** MTC tour 10 (walk) / 20 (drive) and trip 5/15; TM1 skims 20 min for the 2nd boarding, 45/50/60 thereafter; SEMCOG 10/20 (trip 5/15); ARC 0 for walk-transit, 10 (if any transfer) for PNR/KNR; MWCOG 0 (fixed); SANDAG ≈26.5 min IVT for the first transfer (convex 23·(e^0.414n−1) × 2.25); SimOR ≈17.7 for the first transfer; PSRC ASim 21.7–27.2 (work 24.7); DaySim Seattle family 8 min per boarding (incl. first); Chattanooga 2.5; TM2 assignment 10 (example) or 2.5–4.5 per boarding by mode (v05); CMAP 25 min at transfer nodes (assignment). **Count 10 mode-choice models; median ≈10 min IVT per transfer; range 0–27.** A strongly convex (second transfer much costlier) form is used by SANDAG and SimOR; MTC/TM1 differentiate drive-access transfers (20 vs 10).

**Drive-access time:** 2.0× IVT in MTC, SEMCOG, SANDAG (acctime), MWCOG, ARC PNR (2.0; KNR 2.5), DaySim 2.0, CMAP 2; PSRC ASim 1.08–1.5; TM2 v05 assignment 3.0. Median 2.0.

**Rail/LRT-specific treatment.** No model gives a *different walk-access weight* for rail/LRT than for bus — walk weights are identical across all transit sub-modes in every spec read (MTC, SEMCOG, ARC, MWCOG, SANDAG, SimOR, PSRC, DaySim, TM2, CMAP). Rail preference is instead expressed through (a) IVT multipliers: LRT 0.9 (MTC, MWCOG, TM2 v05), 0.85 (SANDAG, DaySim Seattle via −0.15), 0.8 (ARC), 0.75 (DaySim SeaCast/BKRCast via −0.25); heavy rail 0.8 (MTC, MWCOG), 0.75 (ARC), 0.9 (TM2 v05); commuter rail 0.7 (MTC, SEMCOG, MWCOG, TM2 v05), 0.75 (ARC, DaySim), 1.2 (SANDAG); (b) mode-specific boarding penalties in TM2 v05 (LRT 4.5, local bus 4.0, express 2.5, ferry/commuter 0 min); (c) ARC's "MARTA constant" (heavy-rail IVT bonus up to 3 min) and SeaCast's `TransitLightRailPathConstant 0.035`; (d) CMAP's wait perception by node type (bus 2.75 vs rail ≥2.475 vs Brown line 1.0). Station-specific access penalties: none found in any readable file; the only station-type differentiation is SoundCast's undocumented `light_rail_walk_factor = 0.5` / `ferry_walk_factor = 0.5` in accessibility settings (not a mode-choice weight) and SEMCOG's larger walk-access catchment for premium transit (1.2 mi) than local bus (0.85 mi). Walk-access caps: 1.0 mi (DaySim Seattle), 1.5 mi (MTC template, PSRC ASim), 0.85/1.2 mi (SEMCOG), 2.0 mi (Chattanooga), 3 mi / 60 min walk mode (SANDAG).
